A strategy game

Envoy. Keep the world from burning.

Conflicts flare and spread across the map. You have a handful of envoys and not enough time. Win people over, open talks, and get deals signed before the world tips into war.

🗺️ Pick a hotspot🕴️ Spend envoys🙂 Win over parties📜 Sign deals⏭️ End the month

Or name any conflict. CR-AI builds it.

How to win
  • Hotspots heat up every month. At 100 they flare up and spread to linked conflicts. If world tension hits 100, it's over.
  • Parties have a mood. Message them with offers they want 😀. Offer something on their red line and they turn on you 😡.
  • Listen to reveal hidden cards. Dashed cards are CR-AI's guesses from thin sources, and some are wrong.
  • Open talks when enough parties are happy, then propose a deal. Veto parties must sign.
  • Sign the target number of deals before time runs out.
